# Terms: what they must cover
- Not medical or dietetic advice; see a GP before changing diet with a medical condition or medication; not for under-18s,
  pregnancy/breastfeeding or eating-disorder history unless advised by a clinician.
- Allergens: guidance only; always check product labels; "may contain" traces not covered.
- Prices are dated estimates, not live; products may be unavailable or substituted.
- Subscription (DMCC Act): clear pre-contract price/renewal info, free-trial terms and first-charge date, easy online
  cancellation, reminder before renewal where required, cooling-off rights.
- Retailer names/logos belong to their owners; no affiliation unless an agreement says so.
