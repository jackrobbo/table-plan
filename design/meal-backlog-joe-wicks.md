## Notes
- This list leans heavily on chicken (about 11) and pasta bakes. For variety, the best non-chicken picks are the cottage pie, chilli pasta bake, cheesy beef casserole, honey roasted pork noodles and the prawn noodles.
- Beef tacos is already in the app, so skip it or compare ours against his.
- Chilli pasta bake, chilli wraps and the Mob and Morrisons chilli dishes overlap; pick one or two chilli ideas, not four.
- The two gnocchi bakes both need gnocchi; build one or both only if gnocchi will be reused.
- Lamb leg steaks and moussaka push the price up; keep them for a "treat" tier if at all.
