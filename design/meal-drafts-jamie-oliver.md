## What this batch tells us
- Six of the ten take an hour or more, mostly oven time. Jamie's "quick" ones are the 20-minute pair (golden chicken, salmon tacos) and the stir-fry. If the app is about quick, easy weeknights, label oven-heavy tray bakes as "low effort" instead.
- Recipes 1 to 5 add very few new ingredients (about 10 in all), which fits the principle of reusing the catalogue.
- Items that keep coming up and are worth adding once: fresh lime, plain flour, red or white wine vinegar, cannellini or butter beans, white or red cabbage, chorizo.

## Suggested first five to build
1 Spiced chicken & rice traybake, 2 Southern-style sausage stew, 3 Chicken & chorizo bake, 4 Golden chicken & garlicky beans, 5 Zesty salmon tacos. Hold 6 to 9 until the principles are settled. Drop 10.
