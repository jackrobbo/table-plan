## Proposed quality rule (Jack to change freely)
1. **Packaged "protein" snacks need at least 8 g protein per 100 kcal.** This is what separates a protein snack from a chocolate bar with a protein label.
2. **Sugar at most 12 g per serving**, unless it is naturally occurring milk sugar in a yoghurt or milk drink (flagged when above 12 g).
3. **Whole foods (fruit, veg, nuts, eggs) are exempt**: they are judged on being whole foods, and nuts count as a snack in a measured handful (about 30 to 40 g).
4. Labels are copied from the Morrisons page; fresh fruit uses CoFID like the rest of the catalogue.

## What each pick needs to become a snack in the app
- An ingredient entry (name, display name, pack size, regular price, Morrisons slug, per-100 values, allergens), the same as the 173 already there.
- A snack recipe per ingredient (or a pair such as skyr and blueberries) with serving size, macros from the label and cost tier.
- Items already in the catalogue need no new product, only new snack recipes if wanted.
- Allergens from the label: all the dairy items contain milk; the nuts and the Nature Valley bar contain nuts, peanuts or soya. Jerky and biltong labels did not list allergens in the text I read, so check before they go live.
