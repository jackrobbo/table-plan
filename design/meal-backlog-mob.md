## Notes
- 16 of the 26 are chicken. The app needs variety (beef, pork, fish, vegetarian), so pick across proteins when choosing.
- Four are air-fryer recipes. Not every family has one, so label them or offer an oven version.
- Specialist sauces (harissa, gochujang, sriracha, Thai basil) cost one jar for one meal. Only worth it if a sauce is reused across several meals, which is a way to choose: pick one or two sauces and build several meals around each.
- Overlap with batch 1 (Jamie Oliver): chicken chorizo fajitas partly repeats the chicken and chorizo traybake idea.
