129 ingredients are used across all menus: 93 fresh, 36 cupboard. All have verified nutrition except FUEL10K granola, whey isolate and seafood sauce (live meals).

## "Ready" means
1. **Every meal is decided:** live, or removed. No drafts left except deliberate holds (the four NHS lunches until the licence follow-up in `design/todo.md` is done).
2. **Every new meal passes the checks:** method sense, macro sense, original-method outline, scenario runs. Done for all drafts. The 50 older live meals are audited, not gated (77 findings: short methods, no oven temperatures); decide whether to fix them or accept.
3. **Cost tiers decided.** Most new lunches and breakfasts land in "high" (the low and medium cut-offs are about £1.10 and £1.60 for lunch and breakfast).
4. **The ingredient list is frozen** (see below), so cross-store linking is done once.
5. **Photos** are a separate track; meals show an emoji until then.

## Freezing the ingredient list
Why it matters: every fresh ingredient needs a matching product at Tesco, Sainsbury's and Asda. Fewer, more common ingredients mean fewer matches and less waste.

- **Fresh ingredients used by only one meal (11):** porkmince, bagels, lambmince, wafer (Myprotein), cod, greenbeans, pops (Novo), slices (crispy potato), babybel, tomsoup, pouch (baby potato pouch). Each either earns its place or its meal moves to a more common ingredient.
- **Brand-specific items (9):** Grenade bars, Warburtons protein pittas, Mission protein wraps, Myprotein wafer, Novo pops, whey isolate, FUEL10K granola, Mini Babybel, Nando's rub. A brand only works across stores if each store has a close own-brand equivalent; otherwise the meal is Morrisons-only or the item changes.
- **Cupboard staples (36):** handled per store as an optional "only if you're out" list, not matched product by product.
- **Used only by drafts:** turkey slices, protein pittas, wholemeal bread, wafer-thin chicken. These join the linking list once their meals go live.

## Decisions for Jack
1. **Stop adding recipes?** We have about 35 breakfasts, 68 lunches, 80 dinners and 21 snacks including drafts. Lunches are already plentiful. Recommend: finish and lock, then add only to fill specific gaps.
2. **Which drafts go live,** and when (all except the NHS four, recommended).
3. **Cost tiers:** keep, or raise lunch and breakfast cut-offs slightly (recommended).
4. **Branded items:** keep only where each store has a close own-brand equivalent, replace the rest.
5. **The 11 single-use ingredients:** keep or consolidate.
6. **Old live meals:** fix the 77 method findings now, or accept for launch.
7. **Snacks:** in or out of the first release.
