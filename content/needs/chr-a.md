# chr-a: skipped and swaps

Skipped
- Avocado, Tomato & Chicken Sandwich (EatingWell 250881): duplicates chicken-and-avocado-sandwich.
- Buffalo Chicken Grain Bowl (EatingWell 263359): needs buffalo or hot sauce and blue cheese, neither stocked. Suggested: Morrisons Frank's RedHot Buffalo Sauce (or similar) and Morrisons Danish Blue.

Swaps
- Korma: sultanas and sugar left out; ground almonds replaced with mixed nuts.
- Butter chicken: natural yogurt replaced with Greek style; fenugreek, garam masala, chutney and naan left out.
- Chinese chicken curry: turmeric and sugar left out; ground ginger replaced with fresh root ginger.
- Spanish stew: olives left out; white wine vinegar replaced with red wine vinegar; flaked almonds replaced with mixed nuts.
- Satay noodles: wholewheat noodles replaced with fine egg noodles; cumin seeds with ground cumin.
- Prawn harissa spaghetti: cooked king prawns, so heated through not cooked until pink.
- Katsu: almond crust replaced with panko; ready katsu sauce replaces the homemade sauce; basmati instead of brown rice; mint left out.
- Seafood spaghetti: fennel replaced with celery; wholemeal spaghetti with regular; parsley with basil.
- Tostadas: oven-crisped Mission wrap wedges for tostada shells; soured cream for the salad kit dressing; sweetcorn and cheddar for the kit extras.
- Turkey cranberry wrap: plain wrap for light flatbread, soft cheese for whipped cream cheese.
- Caprese sandwiches: farmhouse or wholemeal bread for ciabatta; vinegar for balsamic glaze.
