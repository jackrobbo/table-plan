# lunch-a needs and skips

## Skipped (missing essential ingredient)
- **Chicken, basil & fennel smoosh** (Mob): needs fresh basil and fennel seeds (suggest Morrisons Basil pot and Fennel Seeds jar).
- **Tuna, celery & miso smoosh** (Mob): needs white miso paste, toasted sesame oil and rice vinegar (suggest Morrisons Miso Paste, Sesame Oil, Rice Vinegar).
- **Mustardy chicken & bacon sandwich filler** (Mob): needs Dijon mustard (suggest Morrisons Dijon Mustard), also cornichons.
- **Egg & cress sandwich** (Mob): the watercress pesto is the point; needs watercress (suggest Morrisons Watercress 80g) and Dijon mustard.
- **Leftover ham & brie folded flatbreads** (Mob): needs brie and caramelised onion chutney (suggest Morrisons Somerset Brie and Caramelised Red Onion Chutney).
- **Chicken Caesar chopped salad** (Mob): serves 4, oven croutons plus roast chicken plus blanched broccoli is more than one cook step, and needs anchovies, capers, romaine (suggest Morrisons Little Gem or Romaine hearts, Capers, Anchovies).

## Skipped (duplicate)
- **Pesto pasta salad** (Joe Wicks): same as Chicken Pesto Pasta Salad (pasta, pesto, tomatoes, greens); the original uses vegetarian chicken pieces, which we do not stock.
- **Leftover chicken Caesar wraps** (Mob): same as Chicken Caesar Wrap and Parmesan Chicken Caesar Wrap (also needs croutons and anchovies).
